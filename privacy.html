RuhVerse Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RuhVerse ("we", "our", or "us"). RuhVerse provides a spiritual digital platform available via website (ruhverse.online) and mobile application to help Muslims read the Holy Quran, observe daily prayer times, determine the Qibla direction, and access spiritual reflections.

Your privacy is deeply sacred to us. This Privacy Policy explains clearly and transparently what data we collect, why we collect it, and how your personal data is protected across both our website and mobile application.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Location Information (Prayer Times & Qibla Finder)

RuhVerse requests access to your device's geographical location (latitude and longitude) strictly to:

  • Calculate accurate daily Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) based on your coordinates.
  • Calculate the precise direction of the Kaaba in Makkah for the Qibla Compass.
Privacy Guarantee: Location calculations are performed locally on your device or via anonymous coordinate queries. We never track your movements, store your location history, or share your GPS data with advertisers or data brokers.

B. Account Information (Google Sign-In & Email)

If you create an account or sign in using Google Sign-In or email, we collect:

  • Your name (full name)
  • Your email address
  • Profile picture URL (if provided by Google)
  • A unique account identifier generated by our authentication provider (Supabase)

We use this information exclusively to authenticate you, maintain your active session, and synchronize your personal reading progress across your web and mobile devices.

C. Quran Bookmarks & Reading Progress

When signed in, your saved Quran bookmarks (Surah and Ayah numbers, optional notes) and your last read Ayah position are securely stored in your account so you can resume recitation seamlessly on any device.

3. Third-Party Services & Authentication

To provide high-reliability services, RuhVerse integrates with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers:

  • Google Identity (OAuth 2.0): Enables secure, one-tap sign-in without requiring you to manage a separate password. We only receive standard identity claims (name, email, avatar).
  • Supabase: Provides enterprise-grade PostgreSQL database hosting and identity management with Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring that only you can access your private bookmarks and progress.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize any user data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls to safeguard your data:

  • All data transmitted between your device, the web application, the mobile app, and backend servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Database tables enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies preventing unauthorized cross-user access.
  • Administrative keys and credentials are stored strictly in private server environment variables and never exposed to client applications.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access & Review: You can view all your saved bookmarks, profile information, and reading progress directly in the app or website at any time.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated bookmarks and progress at any time by contacting us at ruhversebusiness@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all account records will be permanently removed.

6. Children's Privacy

RuhVerse is designed for all audiences seeking Islamic knowledge and Quranic recitation. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us for immediate removal.
