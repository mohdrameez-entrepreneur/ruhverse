## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **RuhVerse**. RuhVerse provides a digital spiritual platform via website and mobile application designed to assist Muslims with Quran recitation, Islamic prayer times, Qibla compass direction, and spiritual reflections.

We respect and protect your privacy. This policy outlines how your data is handled across both our website and mobile application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data (Prayer Times & Qibla Direction)
- **Purpose:** To calculate accurate Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and determine the exact Qibla direction towards the Kaaba in Makkah.
- **Privacy Guarantee:** Location coordinates are processed locally on your device or via anonymous coordinate calculations. We **never** track your physical location, save your location history, or share your GPS data with third parties or advertisers.

### B. Account Data (Google Sign-In & Email)
- When you register or sign in via **Google OAuth** or email, we collect:
  - Full Name
  - Email Address
  - Profile Avatar URL (if provided by Google)
  - Unique Account User ID
- **Purpose:** To authenticate your session, maintain account security, and synchronize your bookmarks and reading progress.

### C. Quran Bookmarks & Reading Progress
- Surah number, Ayah number, and optional personal notes.
- Reading position (last read Surah and Ayah) to allow you to seamlessly resume reading on any device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use trusted infrastructure to ensure security and reliability:
- **Google Identity (OAuth 2.0):** For secure Google sign-in.
- **Supabase:** Enterprise PostgreSQL cloud database and authentication provider with strict Row-Level Security (RLS).

We **never** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage
- All network communications are encrypted via HTTPS / TLS.
- Database access is strictly controlled by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies so only the authenticated account owner can view or edit their private data.
- Server secrets and administrative keys are kept strictly in backend environment variables and are never included in public client bundles.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion
- **View Your Data:** You can view all saved bookmarks, profile details, and progress at any time within the app or website.
- **Delete Account & Data:** You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing **ruhversebusiness@gmail.com**. Your records will be deleted immediately upon verification.
